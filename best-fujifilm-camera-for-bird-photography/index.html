Best Fujifilm camera for bird photography? 3 bodies compared by pixels on the bird

Which Fujifilm camera puts the most pixels on a bird? Every Fujifilm body with a guide on this site, on the same 600 mm lens, at the same distances, so the only thing that changes is the sensor.

Short answer: on the same lens, the Fujifilm X-H2 and Fujifilm X-T5 put the most pixels on a bird: about 1,941 across an American robin at 25 m with 600 mm, against 1,567 on the Fujifilm X-H2S. This table measures reach only; autofocus, burst rate and handling matter too and aren't measured here. Each body's guide has its full numbers, from crop headroom to wingtip blur in flight.

What the table shows

On the same lens at the same distance, the bird's image on the sensor is the same size on every body, so reach comes down to how many pixels fit across it: the pixel pitch. The Fujifilm X-H2 and Fujifilm X-T5 have the smallest pixels here (3.04 µm), so they put 1.24 times as many pixels across the bird as the Fujifilm X-H2S (3.77 µm): 1,941 against 1,567 across an American robin at 25 m.

Bodies that share a sensor size and pixel count put exactly the same pixels on the bird with the same lens: the X-H2 and X-T5 (23.5 mm and 7,728 pixels across).

Across all 39 interchangeable-lens bodies with a guide on this site, on the same 600 mm lens, only the Panasonic Lumix G9 II puts more pixels on the bird than the Fujifilm X-H2 and Fujifilm X-T5 (1,970 across the robin at 25 m, against 1,941).

Questions

Which Fujifilm camera puts the most pixels on a bird?

On the same lens, the Fujifilm X-H2 and Fujifilm X-T5 (3.04 µm pixels): about 1,941 pixels across an American robin at 25 m with a 600 mm lens. The Fujifilm X-H2S puts 1,567 across the same robin, so the X-H2 has 1.24 times as many. That's reach only; autofocus, burst rate and handling aren't measured here.

Do the X-H2 and X-T5 put the same pixels on a bird?

Yes, with the same lens and distance: they share a sensor 23.5 mm wide with 7,728 pixels across it (3.04 µm pixels), so each puts 1,941 pixels across an American robin at 25 m with 600 mm. Anything that sets them apart, such as autofocus, burst rate or handling, isn't measured here.
