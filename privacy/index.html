Privacy Policy

BirdFrame — bird photo cropping for Mac and Windows

Last Updated: October 29, 2026
In brief: Your photos are processed on your Mac and are never uploaded by BirdFrame. No BirdFrame account is required. Usage analytics is optional and off unless you agree. Apple handles purchases.

Photos and Files

Bird detection and cropping run locally using models bundled with the app. BirdFrame reads the Photos albums or folders you choose. Photos edits preserve the original; folder exports create new JPEGs. Saved projects and crop settings remain on your Mac. Apple's own iCloud Photos syncing is controlled by your Apple settings.

  • Photos permission: Lets BirdFrame read your selected photos and apply or restore reversible crop adjustments.
  • Folder permission: Lets BirdFrame access the source and export folders you select.
  • Photos Automation permission: Requested when you choose View Album in Photos. It lets BirdFrame read the album reference and ask Photos to reveal that album. This action does not upload, delete, or move your photos.

Optional Usage Analytics

If you choose Share usage data, a configured analytics-enabled version of BirdFrame sends a limited set of usage events to PostHog to help us improve the product. Choosing Not now or skipping the welcome leaves analytics off. The same features remain available either way.

Current status: Analytics transmission is disabled in the current pre-release build. A saved preference alone does not send data. We will complete the hosting, retention and deletion arrangements described below before enabling collection.

  • Actions and outcomes: Source type (album or folder), crop framing choices, processing completion/cancellation/failure, exports, Photos apply/restore outcomes, guide use, and purchase/restore outcomes. Counts and processing times are grouped into ranges.
  • App context: App version/build, release channel, platform, major operating-system version, and free/paid/test access status.
  • Random identifiers: Installation, session and batch IDs help connect related events and understand return usage. They are not derived from your Apple Account, hardware identifiers, photos, or files. This is pseudonymous data, not a promise of irreversible anonymity. We do not link it to a BirdFrame account.

Never included in usage events: Photos, thumbnails, screen recordings, filenames, folder paths, album names, Photos identifiers, photo metadata or GPS coordinates, search text, payment details, purchase receipts, or raw error messages. We do not record sessions, automatically capture arbitrary clicks, sell usage data, or use it for targeted advertising.

PostHog receives events over HTTPS as our analytics provider. We request no person profile or IP-based location enrichment. Like any internet service, PostHog receives the connection's IP address and may process network/security logs; disabling location enrichment does not remove that network metadata. See PostHog's privacy policy. Our project hosting region and applicable provider safeguards will be confirmed and disclosed here before analytics is enabled.

Your Choice and Deletion Requests

Change your choice at any time in Settings → General → Privacy → Share usage data. Turning it off stops new collection, cancels pending requests, and removes the local analytics identifier. Events are not stored on disk for later analytics upload. Activity from before your consent is not uploaded retrospectively.

Turning analytics off does not automatically delete events already delivered to PostHog. Contact birdframe.app@gmail.com to request access or deletion. Because we do not collect your name or email with usage events, we may need an analytics reference to locate them; we cannot promise to identify records from your email address alone. A usable reference/deletion workflow must be in place before collection is enabled.

Retention and Security

No analytics records are currently transmitted. Before enabling analytics, we will configure and verify a maximum retention period of 90 days for raw usage events, publish the hosting region, and establish a way to process deletion requests. Aggregate statistics that cannot reasonably be linked back to an installation may be retained longer. Access to collected usage data will be limited to product improvement and operation of the analytics service. Provider processing must be covered by appropriate data-protection terms.

Your local photos and projects remain until you remove them through the app or your Mac. If you email support, the message and any information you choose to include are received by the support mailbox and used to respond. Avoid including photos or personal files unless you intend to share them.

Purchases

The one-time paid unlock and Restore Purchase use Apple's App Store and StoreKit. No separate BirdFrame account is required. Apple processes payment information. BirdFrame uses verified purchase information to determine access; it does not send receipts, transaction identifiers, Apple Account details, or payment details to PostHog. If you consent to analytics, general purchase outcomes and free/paid status may be collected as described above. See Apple's privacy policy.

BirdFrame for Windows

The sections above describe the Mac app. BirdFrame for Windows works as follows.

  • Photos and files: Bird finding and cropping run on your PC using the models bundled with the app; your photos are not uploaded. BirdFrame reads only the folders you choose in the Windows folder picker and writes crops as new JPEGs in the folder you pick for exports. Your originals are not changed.
  • Stored on your PC: Saved batches, settings and a diagnostic log stay in the app's own storage on your PC and are removed when you uninstall it.
  • No usage analytics: The Windows version sends no usage analytics.
  • Location in crops: Exported crops keep each photo's capture date, camera details and location, unless you turn off Settings → General → Include location in exported crops.
  • Purchases: The Full Version is a Microsoft Store add-on. Microsoft processes payment with your Microsoft account; BirdFrame only asks the Store whether the add-on is owned. See Microsoft's privacy statement.
  • Feedback: Send feedback opens an email in your own mail app with the app version, the Windows version and whether bird finding runs on the graphics card or processor. Nothing is sent unless you send that email.

Website and Waitlists (getbirdframe.com)

This section covers the BirdFrame website, not the app. App usage data described above is never combined with website data.

  • Waitlists: If you join the Windows waitlist or ask us to email you the Mac link, we store your email address, which list you joined, and how you reached the site (described below) in a Google Sheet owned by BirdFrame, and send you that one email. We use your address only for the purpose you signed up for: the Windows list gets one email when BirdFrame for Windows is out. Reply to any email or contact us to be removed.
  • Site measurement: The site records a small set of events (a visit, opening a waitlist, clicking through to the App Store, signing up) with the campaign tags in the link, the referring website's domain, the page, a broad device type (for example iPhone or Windows), your browser's time zone and language, and a random ID that lasts only for your browser session. It does not use cookies for this and does not record your IP address.
  • Meta Pixel: To measure our Facebook and Instagram ads, the site may load the Meta Pixel, which tells Meta about page views, App Store clicks and waitlist sign-ups (not your email address). Meta may use cookies and link this to your Meta account under Meta's privacy policy. Visitors in Europe and the UK are asked first and the pixel stays off unless they choose Allow. Browsers sending a Global Privacy Control signal never load it. You can also manage ad preferences in your Meta account settings.

Google (Apps Script and Sheets) hosts waitlist and site-event records; see Google's privacy policy. We keep site events for up to 12 months and waitlist addresses until the list's purpose is fulfilled or you ask us to remove them.

Children

BirdFrame is a general-audience photo utility, not a service directed at children. We do not knowingly collect analytics from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Policy Changes

We update the date on this page when the policy changes. Material changes to collection will be explained before they take effect, with consent requested where needed. Photo processing does not depend on agreeing to analytics.
