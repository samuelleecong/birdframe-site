About 50 words

Longer

Come home with hundreds of photos and a bird in the corner of each one? BirdFrame finds the birds and crops them in the framing you choose, thousands in one go, from the full-size originals. Nothing is downscaled or generated. Windows works with folders; Mac also works with Apple Photos, where edits are reversible. Folder exports leave the originals untouched. Try up to 50 photos per run free, with unlimited runs. Watch the recorded workshop at https://getbirdframe.com/club-workshop/ or try the browser demo at https://getbirdframe.com/demo/

Email birdframe.app@gmail.com with your club's name. Say where you would share the workshop or member code. A prize licence is available when the Mac code is announced to members. Windows members can try the app from the Microsoft Store. Existing partner agreements continue unchanged.
