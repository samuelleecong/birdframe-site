About 45 words

Longer

Come home with hundreds of photos and a bird in the corner of each one? BirdFrame, a Mac app made by a one-person studio, goes through a whole Apple Photos album or folder, finds the birds, even small, distant or half-hidden ones, and crops each in the framing you pick. Your originals stay untouched. It's free for up to 50 photos at a time, and members get 20% off the full version with code [YOUR CODE]. Try it on one of your own photos in your browser first: https://getbirdframe.com/demo/
