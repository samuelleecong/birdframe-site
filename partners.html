Good to know

Who is it for?

Mac users (macOS 15 or later) with an Apple Photos library or folders of JPEG, PNG or HEIC photos.

Do I have to disclose it?

Yes, please say it's a partner code when you share it, as your platform's rules and the law where you live require.

How are payments made?

Monthly, based on the redemption numbers App Store Connect reports for your code, after Apple's commission and any refunds.

Can my club use it as a fundraiser?

Yes. The 30% goes to the club, and I can add free licences as prizes for a photo contest.
