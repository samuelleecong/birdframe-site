Something useful for your bird photographers

For creators, educators, workshop and tour leaders, festivals and clubs. BirdFrame finds birds and crops thousands of photos in one go from the full-size originals, with no downscaling or generated detail. It is available for Mac and Windows.

Good to know

Who is it for?

Mac users (macOS 15 or later) with Apple Photos albums or folders, and Windows users with folders of photos. Windows users get it from the Microsoft Store.

Do I have to disclose it?

Yes, please say it's a partner code when you share it, as your platform's rules and the law where you live require.

Already a partner?

Your existing agreed terms continue. Email with any questions about your code or reporting.

Can a club offer a prize?

One Mac licence can be arranged for a raffle or photo-contest prize when the club agrees to announce its Mac member code. The prize and code are for Mac; Windows members can use the free tier.
