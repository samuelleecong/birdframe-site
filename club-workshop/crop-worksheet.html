One bird, two crops

Bring one bird photo with room around the subject. Use the crop tool you already have and keep the original handy.

For club hosts

Allow about 30 minutes. Play the 19-minute recording, give everyone 6 minutes to compare their crops, then spend 5 minutes discussing what changed. For a shorter session, play 2:14–5:17 before the exercise.

1. Tell two stories with the same frame

Make a tight crop that shows a field mark or gesture. Make a wider crop that keeps a clue about habitat, scale or behaviour. Record the cropped dimensions before resizing or exporting.

2. Work out the print size

Divide each cropped dimension in pixels by your target pixels per inch. At 300 ppi, a 2,400 × 1,600 crop fits an 8 × 5⅓ inch print at the same shape. For an 8 × 10 inch print at 300 ppi, check for at least 2,400 × 3,000 pixels after matching the print shape. Ask your lab what it recommends.

3. Choose a crop and explain why

Which would you print or submit? Name a detail or piece of context that tipped your decision. If it is a contest entry, check the category and editing rules before exporting.
