How far can you crop a bird photo for printing?

Take one bird photo and try two crops. Calculate the print size for each, then choose the crop that keeps the detail and setting you want.

3 min 40 sec · English captions · Synthetic narration · Made by BirdFrame

Try the exercise

Use any editor. Make one crop that shows the bird’s field marks and another that leaves room for habitat. Divide each side in pixels by your intended print resolution. At 300 pixels per inch, a 2,400 × 3,000 pixel crop makes an 8 × 10 inch print.
